Core Capabilities

Built specifically for Ghana's vegetable value chain

Eliminating produce wastage, payment default, and high transport friction.

Digital Escrow Protection

Buyer funds are safely locked in platform escrow when orders are placed. Money is automatically released to the farmer's Mobile Money wallet only when delivery is verified.

Smart Haulage Matchmaking

Calculates precise distances using Haversine formula and connects farmers with local tricycle (Aboboyaa) operators and KIA Bongo trucks for instant dispatch.

AI Pathology Leaf Scanner

Upload a photo of tomato, pepper, or garden egg leaves to instantly detect pathogens (Early Blight, Leaf Mold, Bacterial Spot) with recommended treatment steps.

Trust Circle Network

Verify connections between smallholders, Kumasi restaurants, and Accra retailers. Build reputation scores and prevent bad actors from entering the network.

Offline USSD Gateway (*920*44#)

Farmers without smartphones or internet access can dial the shortcode to list harvest, check pending orders, and accept transport requests from feature phones.

MoMo & Paystack Integration

Seamlessly top-up escrow balances, request instant withdrawals, or pay via MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, or AT Money directly within the app.
